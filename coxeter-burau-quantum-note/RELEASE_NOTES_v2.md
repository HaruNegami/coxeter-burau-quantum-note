# v2: Quantum gates from rank-one middle convolution on arrangements

This version extends the existing note with Section 7, which constructs
quantum gates from a unitary rank-one seed on an affine hyperplane arrangement
through directional MC. It gives the MC matrices and middle quotient, applies
Negami's invariant-form and signature results, and constructs a common unitary
basis. It includes a sufficient full-unitary-closure/non-Clifford criterion and
a rational finite-image test under the stated base-character hypothesis.

The original Coxeter results and all arrangement appendices remain, with their
domains and conventions distinguished from the MC construction. The main PDF
and TeX retain their filenames; the initial PDF/TeX are in `versions/v1/`.

Added MC code and three examples, including a resonant quotient. Verification
combines symbolic identities, 164 numerical tuples, and exact Galois-signature
checks. This remains an expository and computational working note; checks do
not establish novelty or substitute for mathematical review.
