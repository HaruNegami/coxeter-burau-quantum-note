# Changelog

## v2 — 10 November 2026

- Expanded the existing note, retaining `coxeter_burau_quantum.pdf` and `.tex`.
- Added Section 7: general arrangement → rank-one directional MC → invariant
  form and middle quotient → quantum gates, applying Negami's prior results.
- Added unequal-weight colored transport, exact signature and definiteness,
  a sufficient irrational-phase closure/non-Clifford criterion, and a rational
  finite-image test with finite-image base character.
- Added MC code, three examples, symbolic checks, 164 numerical tuples, and
  exact rational Galois tests, including resonant and inactive-seed cases.
- Added MC input ranks and directional-closure qualifications for Dynkin types.
- Updated the abstract, introduction, attribution table, bibliography, READMEs,
  citation metadata, and Make targets.
- Retained Sections 1–6 and all eight appendices. The appendices have only a
  notation clarification referring to Section 7. All original scripts remain.
- Preserved the initial GitHub-package PDF and TeX in `versions/v1/`.

The Coxeter–Burau and MC families retain their own group domains, dimensions,
parameters and hypotheses; the E-type Coxeter results are not transferred to
the MC family without a comparison theorem.

## v1 — 10 November 2026

Initial package: Coxeter–Burau matrices, forms and gates, E6/E7/E8 closure and
prior-theorem comparison, Dynkin dictionary, and arrangement convolution and
based-monodromy appendices with reproducible code.
